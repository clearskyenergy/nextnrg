Site Revenue Underwriting

BESS Value Stack & Site Revenue Analyzer

Load a site's real consumption — an 8760 interval file or twelve months of utility bills — then dispatch a battery against it. The model enforces a power budget and a cycle budget, so the same megawatt is never sold into two markets at once. You get an annual value stack, a 20-year cash flow, and a straight answer on whether the site is worth building.
